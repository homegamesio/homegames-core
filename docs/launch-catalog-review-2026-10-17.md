# Launch catalog review — October 17, 2026

The collection is reduced from **78 to 32 games**. **46 games (59%)** are marked `isTest: true`; all 12 featured choices in `homegamesio/site.config.json` remain, in the same order. Nothing is deleted.

This is an editorial selection and polish queue. Current code, real gameplay captures and opening/input checks support the choices; they do not prove that strangers will enjoy a complete session. A kept game still needs its listed playthrough and polish. A cut can return after it earns its place with a focused, enjoyable experience.

Source checkouts can show the retained test projects with `TESTS_ENABLED=true`. Website builds exclude them through their existing metadata filter. Native release packaging is a separate acceptance gate.

## Completed in this pass

All 78 candidates opened in offline Chrome; 75 received representative gameplay inputs. Pitch Pot, Snap Judgment and Walkie Talkie only received opening checks and are now tests. These short checks informed selection; they are not complete playthroughs.

All 32 retained games now have current, verified gameplay thumbnails. Hot Potato has a waiting message and corrected late-join layering; the subsequent maintainer-directed polish removed the extra holding/result captions. Its private player views and layering have regression coverage. Moonbounce's Patient Pendulum recording now includes legal follow-up shots for differences between JavaScript engines; all 40 adventure and 15 challenge recordings pass on Node 18 and Node 24 without changing gameplay or granting resources.

The rebuilt website contains 32 games and the original 12 featured selections; all 46 removed detail pages are absent. Core's 129 targeted catalog, metadata, Moonbounce and scoped-frame checks pass, as do 70 website unit checks and all 32 packaged-game browser checks. The browser flow covers private invitations, expiry, Studio copy/export/import and mobile layout. Homepage and About body copy now uses the maintainer's interview answers, with desktop/mobile verification after the final build. See [verification evidence](review-evidence/catalog-editing-2026-10-17/README.md).
