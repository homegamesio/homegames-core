# Weed Smoke Willie

Supply your underwater customers with what they need

A solo ten-minute score run with the original artwork and music. Move the boat and drop supplies onto customers. A run starts with the first gameplay input and ends when its ten-minute clock expires, or when cash is negative and no supplies remain in flight. Play again starts a fresh run.

- Keyboard: A/D or left/right arrows to move, Space to drop, M to toggle sound, Enter to replay after a result.
- Touch/mouse: hold the left, middle or right third of the game to move left, drop or move right. The labeled bottom controls show these areas; the sound and result buttons have their own actions. Landscape is recommended on phones.
- Standard controller: D-pad or left stick to move, primary face button to drop, Select to toggle sound, Start to begin/replay. Physical controllers still need release acceptance.

Each sale increases the combo multiplier. Missing a drop or going 3.5 seconds without a sale resets it. Tier two starts at $5,000 cash and tier three at $50,000; tier three continues until the run ends. Drop costs remain $2/$20/$1,600 and base customer values $5/$30/$2,000. The score is total sales before costs, excluding starting cash.

The game remembers only the best total and sound preference. Reloading opens a fresh run. Failed saves show a retry action. Export the save through the player toolbar to move the record to another installation. The standalone HTML download plays offline.
