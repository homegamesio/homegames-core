# Sandy Castle

A patient beach toy for small hands. Choose a pictured castle, dome, or pointy
mold, scoop sand with the shovel, and pat it into the bucket. Tap any glowing
empty spot to turn out your tower. Build three towers in any order and mix their
shapes and heights however you like. The next suggested mold changes as you build
and after each wave. Every action waits for a fresh tap near its large glowing ring;
holding, keyboard repeats, and taps during an animation cannot skip steps.

When the castle is ready, choose a coral, mint, or lavender flag, then tap the
tower you want to decorate. Choose a shell and tap the sand to put it exactly
where you want it. The selected decoration stays ready for another placement;
flags can be repainted and the fourth shell replaces the oldest of three. The
crab likes a friendly tap too. All these choices stay open-ended and bounded.

The child chooses when to tap **wave!**. A soft wave washes the sandcastle away,
then the shovel is ready again. There are no points, deadlines, or losing states.
The upper-right sound button mutes immediately. All sounds are original quiet
procedural PCM WAV Foley, generated locally with at most three simultaneous voices.

Native Squish 1.5.0 game, landscape 3:2. All art is original vector geometry; no
external assets, fonts, or cross-game dependencies. Licensed under MIT.
