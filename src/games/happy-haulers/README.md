# Happy Haulers

Choose what to build with a smiling dump truck: a **castle**, **bridge**, or **flower garden**. Each pictured project has three deliveries and nine pieces with its own visible geometry. Pick the colors of individual blocks, bridge pieces, pots, and flowers, then watch them become part of your construction. There is no losing, countdown, or reading requirement.

1. Tap one of the three big project pictures.
2. Tap the pictured supplies above the truck to load one piece at a time. Three choices fill the bed; each short loading animation waits for a fresh press.
3. Tap the truck to drive. At the destination, tap the building site under the gold arrow to tip the load into place.
4. Repeat with the next type of building piece. The finished project stays until the child taps **again**, then another project can be chosen.

Keyboard: **1/2/3** choose projects or supplies; **Space/Enter** choose the first option or do the current action. Gamepad face buttons use the same single-step action. Held pointers and keys never queue actions. Off-target taps leave the game waiting patiently.

Upper-right **sound on/off** controls original quiet synthesized WAV effects. Art, particles, cargo, building pieces, and audio voices remain bounded. Portable native Squish 1.5.0; no downloads or outside services.
