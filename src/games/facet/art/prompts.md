# Facet realm artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool for Facet. The PNGs below are the original generated output, copied unchanged into this game. They are embedded by `art-data.js` for native, browser-local, and offline play.

## atelier-v1.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original premium match-three videogame environmental background for Facet. A beautifully polished painterly 3D storybook environment with sophisticated Art Deco crystal architecture and luminous jewel colors, tactile detailed materials, cinematic atmospheric depth. Wide 16:9 landscape composition. The gameplay board overlays the LEFT and CENTER 60% of the frame: keep this region DARK, calm, soft and low contrast with open negative space; put the captivating architectural focal point in the FAR RIGHT and ornate framing along the outer corners. No interface, text, words, letters, logos, gemstones arranged in a grid, characters, or watermarks. This is a background plate, never a UI mockup. Scene: The Luminous Atelier, a magical jeweler's observatory at blue hour. A grand brass and midnight teal octagonal pavilion with amber windows and an immense hanging cut diamond chandelier at far right, curved golden Deco arches, velvet navy sky, tiny distant stars, elegant marble terrace and cut glass prisms at outer corners. Palette midnight navy, deep teal, champagne gold, opal glints. Jewel-box richness and warmly inviting magical discovery.
