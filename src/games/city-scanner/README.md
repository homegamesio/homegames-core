# City Scanner

A little living city to explore together: watch the traffic, feed fish in the lake, stir up a coffee rush, or take a walk through the gardens. There are no timers or scores. Everyone shares the view; up to eight players can explore together.

- Tap a neighborhood at the top, or press **1–5**, to visit it.
- Use the side arrows or **Left/Right** (**A/D**) to move through the city.
- Use **+ / −** (**E/Q**) to zoom. **Home** returns to the lake.
- Tap the sky, lake, park, cafes, buildings, or road to start a little event. **Enter** interacts with the center of the view; **B** calls birds.
- **Pause / Play** or **Space** freezes/resumes the city. You can still explore while paused.

Desktop and landscape tablet screens give the city the most room. This is a canvas game; it does not yet provide screen-reader descriptions of the scene.

From the core repository, run `node tools/city-scanner/preview.js` and open the printed URL. See `docs/city-scanner-revival.md` for the restoration notes and benchmark instructions.
