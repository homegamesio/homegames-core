# Moon Garden

`moon-garden-v2.png` is original environmental art generated for Moonbounce with
the built-in imagegen tool. `../garden-data.js` embeds these exact PNG bytes as
base64 for Homegames browser-local and hosted sessions. The image needs no
external asset server. The game draws interactive pegs, characters, UI, and
effects over the painting.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished 16:9 landscape background painting for an original premium whimsical peg-bouncing arcade game called Moonbounce. Create richly illustrated joyful enchanted moon garden, luminous turquoise blue midnight sky grading into lavender and apricot at the horizon, glowing oversized crescent moon in upper right quarter, puffy sculptural pastel clouds, distant floating islands with whimsical miniature observatory towers, teal curling foliage and glowing peach lantern flowers framing far left and far right edges, rounded crystal formations at lower corners, little gold fireflies. Art direction: exceptionally polished hand-painted 3D storybook casual game environment, soft rounded forms, lovely warm and cool contrast, painterly textures, magical atmosphere, lush saturated colors but readable. Composition crucial: the large LEFT TWO THIRDS central area from x8% to70%, y18% to84% must be a calm mostly-open deep teal-to-blue sky with subtle distant haze, with no busy focal subject there; this is where bright interactive pegs will be overlaid. Put most detailed garden foliage on far edges and lower 10%. The RIGHT quarter has a charming detailed background vignette of a tiny moonlit pavilion on a floating island, with quiet upper area for HUD. Include a flowing cloudbank along lower edge. No game UI, no text, no letters, no numbers, no pegs, no balls, no logos, no watermark. Wide cinematic landscape 16:9. This is environmental game art to be used directly behind live interactive gameplay, not a screenshot or mockup.
